SUCCESS AND SAFETY
Top 10 is an aspiration, never a promised outcome. No vote quota guarantees it.
Measure qualified visitors, real trial activations, useful feedback, setup progress
and customer conversations alongside rank. Start with the actual baseline before
choosing numerical targets; no traffic forecast is established here.
Never ask for upvotes, buy votes/comments, exchange votes, use extra accounts,
join vote rings, scrape contacts or send mass cold messages. Ask people to try the
product and share honest feedback. Product Hunt can remove manipulated launches.
The short preparation window makes audience readiness the largest remaining risk.
