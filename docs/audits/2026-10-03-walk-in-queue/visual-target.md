# Walk-in Queue visual target — 2026-10-03

The redesigned Calendar is the supplied quality reference. Reuse its compact
ClipperDesk shell, Manrope hierarchy, quiet indigo actions, restrained surfaces,
small staff initials and native drawers. The queue gets its own operational
structure: actual numbered sequence, an integrated summary, wait/service hierarchy,
a compact team rail, and current service activity. Phones use deliberate cards.

Initial browser evidence, captured before implementation:
- [Original queue](01-before.png): sparse generic rows, per-row selectors/actions,
  little context about who can serve next, and competing panels.
- [Original check-in](02-before-add.png): a traditional large modal with awkward
  tab/control alignment and no contextual staff fit check.

The target has 78px desktop rows, one primary Serve action, NEXT visible without
relying on position alone, and a calm amber past-estimate cue. Drawer content
progressively reveals contact, notes and history. No hero illustration, giant KPI
cards, invented client flags or extra status badges are introduced.
