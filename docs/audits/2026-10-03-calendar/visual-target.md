# Calendar visual target and initial review

Selected target: evolve the existing ClipperDesk operational shell into a staff-first
schedule. A compact header places location and Add appointment beside Calendar.
A single white toolbar groups Today/arrows/date, search, Day/Team/Week/Agenda and
secondary filters. A slim operational strip exposes bookings, waiting/underway,
and attention. The calendar begins immediately below: 60px sticky time axis,
compact initials/name/working-hours headers, 64px hours, quiet quarter-hour rules,
status-accent cards and hatched off-hours. Details and creation use the established
native drawer. Mobile uses date controls, staff filter and dense chronological rows.

1. `01-before.png`: Day overview is reachable, but controls occupy almost half
   the viewport; no staff availability or quick slot action is visible.
2. `02-before-staff.png`: Staff columns exist, but hours are generic and recurring
   breaks/leave/blocks are not shown; empty white areas falsely look equivalent.

Preserve the brand, existing domain commands, all useful appointment operations,
and location-time behavior. Screenshots establish composition, not accessibility
compliance or backend correctness. Appointment, drawer and mobile observations
will be added during verification.
