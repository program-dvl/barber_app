Each published page has a distinct fit statement, three specific operational
pressures, a four-step representative day, three relevant feature links, two
visible limitations, requirement evidence, breadcrumb, canonical and trial
CTA. There are no local/city routes, stereotypes, stock imagery, customer
claims, regulatory advice or industry-specific demos.
