Definitions: cohort membership is the original successful confirmation date, not
visit or payment date. Replacements retain one logical root; latest outcome/value
is shown once. Later linked collections/refunds update that cohort. “New” means a
distinct client’s first recorded completed visit to this business, without any
cross-business identity/history. Expired sessions are business-wide operational
starts, not unique people or attributed customers. Search Console is profile-wide.

Discovery origin is the first retained non-direct consented source; last source
is recorded separately. Owner shared-link labels are untrusted observations and
booking entry records which route was used. None proves causality. Missing,
expired or withdrawn evidence stays Unknown. Optional evidence expires after
60 days and source proportions may therefore change. Never sum currencies or
call booked value collected revenue. Incremental revenue, rankings, acquisition
lift and competitor superiority remain unproven without separate evidence.
