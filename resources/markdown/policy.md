## About this policy

Clipperdesk provides appointment scheduling and business management software for salons, barbershops and other service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains the personal information handled through our website, business accounts, booking pages and related services. It applies to business owners, administrators, staff, customers booking with a business, and website visitors.

Clipperdesk operates the service. Our operator, postal address and privacy contact are listed in [Contact us](#contact-us). A business using Clipperdesk may also provide its own privacy notice for the services it offers. Please read that notice when booking or providing information to that business.

## Our responsibilities and business customer records

Clipperdesk determines how information is used to operate our website, register and secure accounts, manage our subscriptions and meet our own legal obligations. For these activities, we act as the controller, or the equivalent responsible organization under applicable privacy law.

For appointment records, customer profiles, consultation forms, business messages and other records managed by a business, that business generally determines why the information is collected and how it is used. Clipperdesk processes those records on the business's instructions as its service provider or processor. The business is responsible for its collection notices, permissions and appropriate use of customer and staff information. Payment providers may also determine how they use information for their own fraud prevention and legal obligations.

## Information we collect and its sources

### Business owners and administrators

When you register or use an account, we collect your name, email address, business name, profile information you provide, and account and verification records. Password-based accounts store password hashes. Security settings may include two-factor authentication and recovery information. We keep business memberships, roles and permissions to control access.

Owners and administrators provide business contact details, addresses, locations, opening hours, services, prices, staff assignments, booking policies and other settings. Subscription records include plan and capacity choices, billing name and email, invoices, payment status and limited payment-method details supplied by the payment provider.

### Staff and team members

Businesses provide staff names, contact details, profiles, service qualifications, roles, permissions, working hours, breaks, leave and appointment assignments. Where used, operational records also include commissions, tips and related adjustments. These records support scheduling, access control and business reporting; availability records are not necessarily attendance records.

### Customers booking appointments

You or the business may provide your name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, service preferences, referral information and communication choices. We handle selected services and staff, appointment times and locations, booking history, waitlist requests, cancellations, rescheduling and no-show records. Information also comes from bookings, visits and transactions recorded by authorized staff or imported by the business.

Businesses using consultation forms and client records may collect answers, notes, allergies or sensitivities, treatment preferences, typed-signature evidence, consent wording and versions, timestamps, images and attachments. Some of this information can be sensitive or health-related. Provide only information needed for your service. Businesses must determine the appropriate legal basis and any additional consent needed for sensitive information. Clipperdesk is not an emergency service or an electronic medical record system.

### Website, account activity and communications

We process session and request information, IP addresses, browser or device information, login activity, security events, error information and audit records to run and protect the service. Communication records can include recipient details, message content or template information, delivery status, provider references, replies and consent or suppression records. Information you send to our support or privacy contact is used to handle your request.

Other sources include Google when you choose Google Sign-In, Stripe for billing and transaction updates, and messaging providers for delivery information. These uses are described below.

## Google Sign-In and Third-Party Authentication

### Permissions and information received

Google Sign-In is optional; email and password sign-in is also available. When you choose Google Sign-In, Clipperdesk requests the **openid**, **profile** and **email** permissions. Google supplies your Google account identifier, name, email address and email-verification status. The profile response may also include your profile picture URL and other basic profile fields. Clipperdesk does not save or display the Google profile picture or use those other profile fields in this sign-in flow.

These permissions do not give Clipperdesk access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, contacts or other Google services. We do not receive your Google password.

### How we use and store this information

We use your Google account identifier to recognize your linked Clipperdesk account and your verified email to authenticate you, register your account and prevent conflicting account links. If the verified email matches an existing Clipperdesk account without a different Google link, the accounts can be linked. An existing Google link identifies its Clipperdesk account on later sign-ins. The name is used to prefill registration and becomes your Clipperdesk profile name when registration is completed; you can edit the name before completion.

New users still provide a business name and complete registration. We store the Google identifier and provider name with the account link, and the name and email with your account. Google Sign-In preserves any two-factor challenge already configured for your account.

OAuth credentials returned during sign-in are used to obtain the authentication response. Clipperdesk does not persist Google OAuth access or refresh tokens. An unfinished Google registration has a ten-minute validity window in the server-side session. Expiry prevents completion of that registration; it is not a guarantee that all session or backup records are immediately erased.

### Retention, sharing and limits on use

Google account links and account profile information are kept while needed to provide your account and authenticate access. Account closure or a privacy request is reviewed using the retention criteria in [Retention and deletion](#retention-and-deletion). We do not have automatic deletion of every account-related record.

Hosting and storage providers may process the stored account information to operate Clipperdesk. Your account name and email may also be used for account emails, workspace membership and subscription billing when you use those features. Authorized administrators of your business can see the account information needed to manage team access. The Google account identifier is not sent to advertising providers or used as payment or messaging metadata in these integrations. Necessary security or legally required disclosures are described in [Sharing and service providers](#sharing-and-service-providers).

We use Google authentication information for account access and the associated account operations described here. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, credit decisions or unrelated profiling, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence models. Clipperdesk's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

### Disconnecting Google and requesting deletion

You can remove Clipperdesk's connection through your [Google Account connections settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revoking access stops future use of that authorization. It does **not** delete your Clipperdesk account, remove information already stored by Clipperdesk, end your subscription or sign you out of an existing Clipperdesk session. Choosing Google Sign-In again can authorize a new connection.

Clipperdesk does not currently offer a self-service Google unlink control. To request removal of your Google account link, account closure or deletion of account data, contact **{{privacy_email}}** from your account email and specify your request. We verify identity and explain any records that must be retained. If you need to continue using your account without Google, use the email password-reset option to establish access before removing the connection.

## How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- Register and verify accounts, manage workspace access, and protect accounts against misuse.
- Configure businesses, locations, services, staff, permissions and availability.
- Create and manage appointments, waitlists, customer records, forms and consent records on the business's instructions.
- Manage subscriptions, invoices, payment reconciliation, receipts, taxes and recorded refunds or adjustments.
- Deliver account, security, billing and appointment messages, and record delivery or reply information where enabled.
- Provide available exports and reports, handle support and privacy requests, investigate failures and maintain security and transaction history.
- Comply with applicable law, resolve disputes and protect the service and the people using it.

Required fields are identified in the relevant form. Without necessary account or booking information, we may be unable to provide the requested service. Business marketing messages require the applicable permission; appointment or security messages serve separate operational purposes. Google sign-in authorization does not constitute consent to marketing.

Where EU or UK data protection law applies to our own processing, our grounds may include performing our contract with you or taking requested steps before a contract; legitimate interests in securing accounts, preventing abuse and maintaining reliable services; compliance with legal obligations; and consent where required for optional communications or other optional processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests or withdraw consent as applicable. Businesses determine the grounds for their own customer and staff records, including any additional condition required for sensitive data.

## Payments and billing

**Clipperdesk subscriptions:** Stripe processes subscription payments and provides hosted checkout and billing management. We send billing contact information and transaction details needed for these services. We store subscription and invoice references, amounts, currencies, tax and payment status, credits or refund evidence, and limited payment-method descriptors such as type, last four digits and expiry where Stripe supplies them.

**Appointment and business payments:** These are separate from your business's Clipperdesk subscription. Businesses can record payments, deposits, receipts, taxes, refunds and adjustments in their records. Where an enabled payment flow uses Stripe, Stripe processes the payment details and Clipperdesk handles transaction identifiers, amounts, currency, status and related business or appointment information. Availability depends on the business and payment flow; a recorded payment or refund is not itself evidence that money was transferred by a provider. A booking page will identify any payment action actually available.

Clipperdesk does not store full card numbers, card security codes or online banking credentials entered into Stripe-hosted payment interfaces. Stripe also processes information for its own payment security and legal purposes under the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). For appointment charges and refunds, contact the business providing the service. For Clipperdesk subscription questions, contact us. See our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy) for the separate policy and its current status.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information according to its purpose, our relationship with you, business instructions and applicable legal obligations. Retention takes account of whether information is needed to provide the service, maintain accurate transaction or consent history, investigate security issues, resolve disputes or meet accounting and other legal requirements.

- **Account and Google authentication records:** retained while needed to operate and secure your account, subject to review when closure, unlinking or deletion is requested.
- **Account login history:** configured for routine cleanup after {{login_history_days}} days. Other security and audit records may remain where needed for the purposes described above; this is not a universal expiry period for all security records.
- **Business, staff and customer records:** retained for the business's service and recordkeeping purposes. The business controls its collection and requests changes through available tools or assistance.
- **Invoices, payment adjustments, consent and audit evidence:** may remain after other records are removed where needed for legal obligations, security, disputes or reliable historical records. These records are not silently rewritten when a subscription ends.
- **Exports:** client privacy-export files are marked for a 30-day availability period and use short-lived download links. That marker is not a promise of automatic physical deletion. Subscription termination records an export-eligibility window of {{termination_export_days}} days, subject to access permissions and available export functions. Request an export before ending service; no complete automatic business export is promised.
- **Backups and archives:** we cannot promise immediate removal from every backup or archive. A request must be reviewed against the applicable storage, backup and legal-hold arrangements; we do not state an unverified backup expiry period.

Canceling a subscription ends or schedules the end of paid service under the billing terms. It does not erase customer records, close every user account or revoke Google access. Account closure and customer record deletion are separate requests.

The current customer-record deletion workflow records and reviews a request but does not automatically erase or anonymize the records. Complete automated account-data deletion and a record-by-record disposal schedule are not available. Contact us or the controlling business to request review and understand what can be removed, what must be retained and why. We do not promise immediate or permanent deletion across all records. Where deletion is legally required, a technical limitation does not remove that obligation.

## Your choices and privacy rights

Contact **{{privacy_email}}** for information we control, including registration, Google account links, account security and subscription records. Include your account email, the relevant business and the action you request. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or sensitive consultation details in your initial message. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity and authority before releasing or changing records.

For appointment history, consultation information and other records controlled by a business, contact that business first using its booking or service contact details. We can assist the business with available access, correction, export and consent-withdrawal functions. An available export may omit internal security information, staff notes or records requiring a separate review; contact the business for a complete assessment of your request.

Account holders can change available profile and security settings. A valid appointment management link allows the changes offered for that booking. Ask the business to change communication choices or use the unsubscribe control where provided. Withdrawing consent affects future consent-based processing and does not require removal of records supported by another lawful ground.

Depending on the law that applies, your rights may include:

- **EU and UK:** access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to your competent supervisory authority. Contact us about our controller processing or the business about its customer records. You may contact the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or find an EU authority through the [European Data Protection Board](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
- **United States:** applicable state rights to know or access information, obtain a copy, correct it, request deletion, opt out of specified processing, and receive equal treatment for exercising rights. Authorized agent requests and appeals are handled where required by applicable law. California rights concerning sale, sharing or certain uses of sensitive information apply where the law covers the processing; our current service does not sell information or use it for behavioral advertising. Contact us to submit a request or appeal a decision.
- **Canada:** access, correction, information about our practices, withdrawal of consent subject to applicable limits, and challenging compliance. Contact us with a concern; you may also contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/) or the relevant provincial privacy authority.
- **India:** where the applicable provisions are in force and apply to the processing, rights may include information about personal-data processing, correction, completion, updating, erasure, consent withdrawal, grievance redressal and nomination. Contact us for a request or grievance and information about the applicable escalation process.

Rights depend on applicable law and may have exceptions. We assess requests individually and respond within applicable legal time limits. We do not charge for or discriminate against lawful requests except where a permitted fee or other exception applies. No universal statutory deadline or compliance certification is claimed.

## Security

We use account verification, permission checks, business-scoped access controls, expiring protected links and audit records. Specified sensitive client fields are encrypted by the application and private client attachments require authorized access. These safeguards do not imply that every field is separately encrypted or that any certification has been obtained.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Our [Security page](/security) describes current safeguards and their limitations. Protect your sign-in details, use available two-factor authentication and contact us about suspected account misuse. Businesses should restrict staff access to the information needed for their work.
